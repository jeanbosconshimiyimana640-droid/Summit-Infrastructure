About Us

One partner for survey, design and construction

Summit Infrastructure Ltd is a Rwandan engineering and geospatial consultancy. We combine civil engineering, land surveying and GIS, so clients deal with one accountable team from the first measurement on site to the finished structure.

Our name reflects our standard: reach the highest point of quality on every project through precise data, checked designs and disciplined delivery.

Our Vision

To be a trusted leader in infrastructure and geospatial services in Rwanda and the region.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, safe and cost-effective engineering and survey solutions that support national development.

Our Values

Integrity, precision and quality in everything we measure and build.

What We Do

Our Services

Ten core services that cover the full project cycle.

Civil Engineering & Construction

Planning, supervision and delivery of civil works, from site preparation to completion.

2

Land Surveying & Setting Out

Accurate boundary, control and construction setting-out using total stations and GNSS.

3

Topographic & Cadastral Surveying

Detailed terrain surveys, parcel demarcation and cadastral plans.

4

GIS & Remote Sensing

Spatial analysis, satellite and drone image processing, and decision-support maps.

5

Road Design & Infrastructure

Geometric design, drainage and layout of roads and supporting infrastructure.

6

Building Design & Construction

Architectural and structural design with construction supervision.

7

Quantity Surveying

Bills of quantities, cost estimates, valuations and contract cost control.

8

Geotechnical Investigation

Site and soil investigation to guide safe, economical foundation design.

9

Mapping & Spatial Data Management

Digital maps, geodatabases and well-organised spatial data.

10

Project Management

Scheduling, budgeting, quality control and reporting through to hand-over.
